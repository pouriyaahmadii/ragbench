# Employee Handbook

## Welcome to Our Company

Welcome to our organization! This handbook provides an overview of our policies, benefits, and expectations.

## Employment Policies

### Equal Employment Opportunity
We are committed to providing equal employment opportunities to all employees and applicants without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or genetic information.

### Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to conduct themselves professionally and ethically. This includes:
- Treating colleagues with respect
- Maintaining confidentiality of sensitive information
- Avoiding conflicts of interest
- Complying with all applicable laws and regulations

### Attendance and Punctuality
Regular attendance and punctuality are essential. Employees should notify their supervisor as soon as possible if they will be late or absent.

## Compensation and Benefits

### Pay Schedule
Employees are paid bi-weekly on Fridays. Pay periods run from Sunday to Saturday.

### Health Insurance
We offer comprehensive health insurance options including medical, dental, and vision coverage. The company pays 80% of premiums for employee coverage and 60% for dependents.

### Retirement Plan
We offer a 401(k) plan with company matching up to 4% of eligible compensation. Employees are eligible after 90 days of service.

### Paid Time Off
- Vacation: 15 days per year (prorated for partial years)
- Sick Leave: 10 days per year
- Holidays: 10 company-observed holidays per year
- Personal Days: 3 days per year

## Workplace Policies

### Remote Work
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Full-time remote work requires VP approval.

### Technology Use
Company equipment and systems are for business use. Personal use should be minimal and not interfere with work responsibilities.

### Security
All employees must complete annual security awareness training. Report security incidents immediately to IT Security.

## Leave Policies

### Family and Medical Leave
Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave under FMLA for qualifying reasons.

### Bereavement Leave
Up to 5 days of paid leave for immediate family members.

### Jury Duty
Paid time off for jury duty service.

## Performance and Development

### Performance Reviews
Formal performance reviews are conducted annually. Mid-year check-ins are encouraged.

### Professional Development
The company supports professional development through:
- Tuition reimbursement (up to $5,000/year)
- Conference attendance (with manager approval)
- Internal training programs
- Mentorship program

## Separation of Employment

### Resignation
Two weeks' notice is requested for voluntary resignation.

### Termination
Employment may be terminated at will by either party. Final pay includes accrued but unused PTO.
