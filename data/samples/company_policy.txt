# Company Data Retention Policy

## Purpose
This policy establishes the requirements for retaining and disposing of company data in compliance with legal, regulatory, and business requirements.

## Scope
This policy applies to all data created, received, or maintained by employees, contractors, and third-party service providers in the course of company business.

## Retention Periods

### Financial Records
- Tax returns and supporting documents: 7 years
- Financial statements and audit reports: Permanent
- Accounts payable/receivable records: 7 years
- Payroll records: 7 years

### Human Resources Records
- Employee personnel files: Duration of employment + 7 years
- Recruitment records: 3 years
- Training records: Duration of employment + 5 years
- Benefits enrollment: Duration of employment + 6 years

### Legal and Compliance Records
- Contracts and agreements: Duration of contract + 7 years
- Litigation holds: Until legal counsel authorizes destruction
- Regulatory filings: Permanent
- Intellectual property records: Permanent

### Information Technology Records
- System access logs: 2 years
- Security incident reports: 5 years
- Backup logs: 90 days
- Network configuration: 3 years

## Disposal Methods

### Electronic Records
- Secure deletion using DoD 5220.22-M standard
- Certificate of destruction required for sensitive data

### Physical Records
- Cross-cut shredding (P-4 security level minimum)
- Incineration for highly sensitive documents
- Certificate of destruction required

## Roles and Responsibilities

### Data Owners
- Classify data according to sensitivity
- Define retention periods for their data domains
- Approve disposal of records

### Records Management Team
- Maintain retention schedules
- Execute approved disposal
- Maintain certificates of destruction
- Audit compliance quarterly

### Employees
- Follow retention guidelines for their work products
- Report potential violations to Records Management
- Do not destroy records under litigation hold

## Enforcement
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Legal violations will be reported to appropriate authorities.

## Review and Revision
This policy will be reviewed annually by the Records Management Team and Legal Counsel. Changes require approval from the Chief Compliance Officer.
